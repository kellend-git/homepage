Prospect Pipeline

From a book of accounts to a shortlist a seller can act on.

For each sales play, the pipeline looks across account conversations, CRM notes, and product activity for a specific customer problem. It gives sellers a ranked shortlist with the evidence behind each pick and a question to ask next.

Prospect Pipeline · Technical details

Search broadly, then read closely

A broad search finds plausible matches for a sales play. A deeper review brings the relevant records together for each candidate so the shortlist reflects the quality of the evidence as well as the apparent fit.

  1. 1. Retrieve

    Search permitted, read-only sources for relevant passages, account notes, and usage signals.

  2. 2. Assess

    Read the strongest candidates in more detail. Record supporting evidence, gaps, and conflicting signals.

  3. 3. Rank and share

    Apply the play’s rubric, run quality checks, and share the ranked list with source-backed briefs.

What shapes the ranking

Fit
Does the account’s situation match the play?
Customer pain
How clearly has the customer described the problem?
Potential impact
How much could solving it change the customer’s workflow?
Readiness
Is there recent evidence that the customer can act on it?

A model’s assessment feeds the rubric alongside the evidence. Weak or stale evidence lowers priority, and links in each brief let the seller check the reasoning against the original record.

Checks before sharing

Hold a refresh for review if the shortlist changes unexpectedly or the evidence is too thin. Keep prior runs available for comparison. The seller decides whether to follow up and what to say.
